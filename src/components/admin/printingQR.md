For printing thick, adhesive table cards, the required hardware depends on whether you are using pre-cut adhesive label sheets or a thermal label printer:

1. The Ideal Option: Thermal Label Printer (e.g., Rollo, Dymo, Brother, or Munbyn)
How it works: These printers use direct thermal printing on rolls of continuous or pre-sized thermal sticker paper.

Paper / Material to use: Direct Thermal Synthetic (Polypropylene/Vinyl) Sticker Rolls or Thick Matte White Thermal Adhesive Labels (typically sized around 100mm x 150mm / 4x6 inches).

Why it's best:

The material is waterproof, scratch-resistant, and tear-proof, which is essential for cafe tables where drinks might spill.

It has a strong adhesive backing so you can peel and stick it directly onto acrylic table stands, wooden surfaces, or table edges.

It requires zero ink or toner (thermal printing).

2. The Alternative: Standard Laser Printer
How it works: If you are using a regular office laser printer instead of a thermal printer.

Paper / Material to use: A4 Sticker Paper Sheets (Glossy or Matte Vinyl/PP) or Heavyweight Adhesive Cardstock (around 200–250 gsm). You can print the design onto the full sheet and cut out the cards individually.

Why use it: Great if you want a thicker, rigid cardstock feel (like a plastic-feel menu card) that you can slip into double-sided acrylic table tents.
